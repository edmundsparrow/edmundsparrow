Hi, I'm Edmund Sparrow.

I'm a solo developer based in Nigeria, building the Gnoke ecosystem — an offline-first stack of vanilla JavaScript, PHP, and SQLite. No frameworks, no build steps, no dependency on the cloud staying up.

I call myself a "vibe engineer" — I architect from first principles rather than a formal CS background, and I build the entire ecosystem from an Android phone. Everything here, including this CMS, is proof that lightweight tooling can still be capable tooling.
